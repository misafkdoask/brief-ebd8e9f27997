# AEO/GEO Services Market Deep-Dive (July 2026)

## 1. Market size and growth

- The GEO/AEO software+services market is estimated at **$848M (2025) → ~$1.1-1.5B (2026)**, projected to **$17-20B by 2034** at 40-46% CAGR, per multiple 2026 market reports ([Marketintelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/generative-engine-optimization-geo-market): $1.48B 2026 → $17.02B 2034 @ 45.5% CAGR; [Dimension Market Research](https://dimensionmarketresearch.com/report/generative-engine-optimization-geo-market/) @ 40.6% CAGR; [Superlines](https://www.superlines.io/articles/how-big-of-a-market-is-generative-engine-optimization-geo)). Note: these are low-tier research houses; treat as directional, but all point the same way.
- Underlying driver: AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, AI Overviews) now process an estimated **15B+ AI-generated queries/month** (early 2026), and **60% of commercial research queries are forecast to be AI-assisted by end-2026** ([Gupta Deepak market research](https://guptadeepak.com/generative-engine-optimization-geo-market-research-industry-analysis-2026/), [AdventurePPC](https://www.adventureppc.com/blog/small-business-ai-adoption-in-2026-why-the-ai-for-main-street-act-is-the-tipping-point)).
- Traffic quality flipped in 12 months: AI-referral traffic that converted **38% worse** than non-AI in Mar 2025 now converts **42% better** (Mar 2026); Similarweb pegs AI-referral conversion at **7.1% — second only to paid search (7.8%) and ~2.5x Google organic** ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-traffic-converts-42-percent-better-2026-channel-strategy), [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/), [Pixis](https://pixis.ai/blog/why-ai-search-traffic-converts-at-4-5x-what-the-data-actually-shows/)). This is the core ROI argument sellers use.
- The referral market is fragmenting: ChatGPT's share of AI referrals fell from 84.2% (Apr 2025) to **76.9% (Apr 2026)**, with Gemini 9.0%, Perplexity 7.7%, Copilot 3.8%, Claude 2.7% ([Goodie 2026 AI Search Traffic Report](https://higoodie.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-report-2026/)). Fragmentation is good for service sellers — multi-engine tracking is harder DIY.

- Conductor's **"State of AEO/GEO in 2026" CMO Investment Report** (250+ enterprise executives, published Jan 2026): **94% of enterprises plan to increase AEO/GEO spend in 2026**; enterprises already allocate **~12% of digital marketing budgets** to AI search visibility; **97% report measurable positive business impact**; AEO/GEO ranked the **#1 strategic marketing priority for 2026** ([Conductor](https://www.conductor.com/academy/state-of-aeo-geo-report/), [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260113439299/en/), [CMS Critic](https://cmscritic.com/in-the-race-to-ai-visibility-are-aeo-and-geo-really-delivering-97-of-digital-leaders-say-yes)).
- VC validation: **Profound raised a $96M Series C at a $1B valuation (Feb 2026)**, total $155M from Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/exclusive-ai-threatens-search-profound-130000513.html), [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/profound/__H4PHyRvqt_45ZMWcgQxtbYPTDXCmJlYVYuwTi5SF9NU)). **Peec AI: $29M raised, $4M+ ARR in 10 months** — fastest-growing challenger. The AI-visibility tool category raised **$300M+ between mid-2025 and spring 2026** ([Surmado](https://www.surmado.com/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools-2026), [Industry Lens](https://industry-lens.com/reports/ahrefs-brand-radar-semrush-ai-toolkit-adobe)).
- Case studies with revenue attached: Discovered Labs B2B SaaS — **€64K closed revenue from €16.5K spend (3.9x) in one quarter**; Netpeak ecommerce — +120% AI-driven revenue, +693% AI-channel visits; iPullRank financial-services client — +52% traffic, +121% sign-ups; Rankio — +38% AI share-of-voice in 6 months ([Discovered Labs](https://discoveredlabs.com/blog/case-study-how-a-b2b-saas-used-a-geo-agency-to-3x-citation-rates-in-90-days), [Ranktracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/best-geo-agencies-2026/), [Rankio](https://www.rankio.studio/learn/case-studies)).

## 6. How hard is delivery?

**Moderately hard, but tractable — and mostly repackaged SEO/PR craft:**
- Evidence-backed levers ([Radiant Elephant's evidence review](https://www.radiantelephant.com/geo-tactics-what-works-evidence-based-research-review/), [Quattr](https://www.quattr.com/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-llms), [Frase playbook](https://www.frase.io/blog/what-is-generative-engine-optimization-geo)): answer-first structured content (headings, tables, explicit definitions); statistics (+22% citation likelihood) and direct quotes (+37%, from the original Princeton GEO paper); entity schema (sameAs/about/mentions) as hygiene; content freshness (matters especially for Perplexity); and **off-site presence — Reddit was the single most-cited domain in a 150K-citation analysis (40.1%)**, plus Wikipedia, YouTube, review sites and listicles ([Viewership on Reddit GEO](https://viewership.ai/blog/how-to-use-reddit-for-geo), [Brainz Digital](https://www.brainz.digital/blog/reddit-seo-llm-aeo/)).
- Weak/speculative levers: **llms.txt has no confirmed impact** ("adopt because it's low-cost, not because it's proven" — Kevin Indig); Google's own May 2026 guide explicitly stated there is **no special file/markup that earns AI visibility** and that AI Overviews/AI Mode run on the same ranking systems as Search ([SEOteric on Google's myth-busting](https://www.seoteric.com/geo-myths-debunked-what-seos-should-really-do-about-llms-txt-schema-and-freshness/), [dev.to technical review](https://dev.to/geobuddy/llmstxt-schema-markup-and-technical-geo-what-actually-works-in-2026-o63)).
- Time-to-results: first citations in **4-6 weeks**, visible results **2-3 months**, real ROI horizon 6-12 months (consistent across EN and RF sellers). Some outliers claim much faster (6.8%→46.7% AI visibility in 7 days on a narrow query set — [Jaeckert O'Daniel](https://www.jaeckert-odaniel.com/en/geo-case-study-46-7-ai-visibility-in-7-days/)), typically for low-competition niches.
- Hard parts: **measurement volatility** (same prompt yields different answers; cross-engine variance far exceeds Google-vs-Bing variance), attribution, and platform churn — a legitimate skeptic literature calls pure-play GEO "snake oil" when sold as tricks ([Webbiquity](https://webbiquity.com/ai-in-marketing/beware-the-generative-engine-optimization-snake-oil/)). Honest positioning = "GEO is SEO+digital-PR+content engineering with new measurement," which favors sellers who can actually ship content and schema, not just dashboards.

## 7. Is this a good wedge for a 2-person shop? Assessment

**Yes — best wedge among the venture's service lines**, with caveats:
- **Pro:** genuine urgency narrative backed by enterprise data (94%/97% Conductor numbers close deals); productized entry offer exists ($1.5-5K audit, delivered in days with $29-99/mo tooling + Claude-Code-driven analysis pipelines — plays directly to Mikhail's automation strength); tripwire-audit→retainer path is the documented conversion motion; long tail of SMB/mid-market buyers is unserved; in RF/CIS the specialist field is still SEO-agencies-with-a-landing-page, and Mikhail already has an internal `aeo-audit` skill/asset. Deliverables (structured content, schema, Reddit/PR seeding, monitoring reports) are automatable to a degree competitors' manual shops are not.
- **Con:** crowded claims layer (60+ agencies, every SEO shop rebranding); results take 2-3+ months, which strains a 90-day validation window — sell the **audit** (paid, fast, self-contained) as the validation product, not the retainer; measurement volatility creates churn risk; Google actively debunks GEO-specific tactics, so overclaiming is reputationally dangerous — the venture's "only where it actually helps" framing fits well; RF price points (60-100K ₽/mo) mean the EN market pays 3-5x more for the same work, arguing for EN-first with RF as a volume/testing ground.
- **Fastest validation path suggested by the data:** productized AEO/GEO audit at $1,500-2,500 (EN) / 50-80K ₽ (RF) targeting B2B SaaS and fintech/crypto (where founders already have network), delivered on Otterly/Profound-entry tooling + custom pipeline, with a case-study-grade before/after measured on a fixed 50-100 prompt set — converting 1-2 audits into $2.5-5K/mo retainers hits the 90-day goal.

## Key takeaways
- Market is real and early: GEO/AEO market ~$1.1-1.5B in 2026, projected $17-20B by 2034 (40-46% CAGR); AI platforms handle 15B+ queries/month.
- Enterprises are already paying: Conductor (Jan 2026, 250+ execs) — 94% increasing AEO/GEO spend, ~12% of digital budgets allocated, 97% report measurable impact, #1 marketing priority for 2026.
- VC validation is loud: Profound hit $1B valuation ($96M Series C, Feb 2026); Peec AI $4M+ ARR in 10 months; $300M+ poured into AI-visibility tools in ~9 months; Adobe acquired Semrush.
- Pricing benchmarks: audits $1.5-5K one-time; retainers $3-15K/mo (EN); enterprise agencies (iPullRank, First Page Sage) $10-50K/mo. Russia: 60-100K ₽/mo entry, tiers to 220K ₽/mo — EN pays 3-5x more.
- Tools are cheap inputs, not competitors: Otterly from $29/mo, Peec $95/mo, Profound $99/mo entry — monitoring is commoditizing; money is in interpretation + implementation, which tools don't do.
- Delivery is repackaged SEO/PR + content engineering: proven levers are answer-first structure, stats/quotes (+22%/+37% citation lift), entity schema, freshness, and off-site (Reddit = 40.1% of LLM citations). llms.txt unproven; Google publicly debunked 'magic file' tactics — avoid overclaiming.
- Time-to-results 2-3 months (ROI 6-12 mo) strains the 90-day validation goal — so the validation product must be the paid audit (deliverable in days), converting into $2.5-5K/mo retainers via the documented 'tripwire audit → retainer' motion.
- Competition is crowded in claims but shallow in track record: 60+ listed agencies globally, RF market is SEO shops that bolted GEO on in 2025 — no dominant specialist in either market yet; automation-heavy delivery is a real differentiator for a 2-person shop.
- Demand exists down-market too: live Upwork jobs for AEO/GEO consultants and paid one-page GEO audits; 40-45% of SMBs haven't started with AI and 60% lack in-house expertise — an explicit adoption-gap opportunity.
- Verdict: AEO/GEO is the venture's best wedge — EN-first (price premium), B2B SaaS + fintech/crypto ICP (existing network), productized audit as entry, honest 'GEO is SEO+PR done right' positioning to dodge the snake-oil backlash.
