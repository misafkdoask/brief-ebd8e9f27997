## ICP #1 — Crypto/Fintech B2B Infrastructure & Platforms (Global EN) — HOME TURF, LEAD ICP

**Segment:** Crypto exchanges/wallets/on-ramps, B2B crypto infrastructure (custody, payments, compliance tooling), fintech SaaS. 20–200 employees, seed–Series B, HQ Dubai/EU/UK/remote-global (incl. CIS-founded global companies). Marketing team of 1–3 people.

**The pain (verified, structural, worsening):**
- **Paid acquisition is legally choked.** Google bans ads for ICOs, DeFi protocols, and crypto trading outright; exchanges/wallets need certification, and as of 2026 EU crypto ads are restricted to MiCA-authorized CASPs only; Meta introduced a three-tier authorization system in March 2026 (acquire.fi, support.google.com, thekollab.io). Result: organic content, PR, community and AI-search visibility are not "nice to have" — they are the acquisition channel.
- **AI search is now a named, measured battleground in this vertical.** 5WPR publishes a Crypto AI Visibility Index; Coinbase + Kraken capture ~22% of all US crypto-category AI citations, ChatGPT recommends Coinbase in 85% of exchange queries (prnewswire.com, financefeeds.com). Everyone below the top-5 is effectively invisible in the channel where users increasingly start — and knows it (industry press: "40% of crypto projects will lose users to AI search in 2026," ventureburn.com).
- **Content velocity vs compliance bottleneck.** Fintech content goes through legal review as a final gate; 68% of marketing teams using GenAI at scale hit QA bottlenecks within two quarters (billricestrategy.com, digitalapplied.com). A workflow that bakes compliance checks INTO the pipeline is a differentiated deliverable Mikhail can actually build.

**Buyer:** Head of Marketing / CMO / founder-CEO (at seed, the founder IS the buyer). At EMCD-type companies, often a single marketing lead drowning in channels.

**Budget they control:** Crypto marketing retainers run $3-6K/mo entry-level, $10-50K/mo full-service, token launches $100K+ (luvkaizen.com, apcollective.io). A $2.5-8K/mo specialist line item (AEO/content-engine/automation) is small relative to what they already pay agencies — and far below the $20K+ full-service floor.

**Why a 2-person shop wins here:** (a) Domain credibility — big AI agencies won't touch crypto or don't understand MiCA/certification constraints; generic agencies produce compliance-unsafe copy. (b) Warm trust — crypto hiring/buying runs on Telegram and personal networks, not RFPs. (c) Price — nothing exists between $99/mo monitoring tools and $10K+ crypto agencies. (d) Both founders have shipped marketing inside a crypto company (EMCD, Choise/Vault) — the "we've sat in your seat" story.

**$0 reach:** EMCD/Choise alumni network + LinkedIn 1st/2nd degree in crypto (Mikhail's reach system + person-map already built); Telegram crypto-founder/marketer chats; conference-parse targeting (Token2049 Singapore Oct 2026, Dubai events); existing aeo-audit skill → free/cheap teardown of a target's AI-visibility as the door-opener DM.

**Honest downside assessment (why it's still #1 anyway):** cyclical budgets tied to token prices; startup mortality → churn risk on retainers; scam-adjacent inbound requires client filtering (verify-person workflow exists); some sub-verticals (memecoins, degen DeFi) are reputationally toxic — restrict ICP to licensed/infra/B2B players. None of this outweighs the decisive advantage: it is the only segment where a first client in 2-6 weeks via warm outreach is realistic, which is what the 90-day validation goal demands.

**Entry offer:** paid AI-visibility + content-ops audit $1.5-3K → "AEO/GEO system + compliant content engine" at $3-5K setup + $2.5-5K/mo.

## ICP #2 — B2B SaaS, Seed–Series B (Global EN) — SCALE ICP

**Segment:** B2B SaaS, 10-100 employees, $1-20M ARR, US/EU/remote. Marketing = 1 generalist or founder-led. Adjacent-to-home-turf slice: fintech-SaaS and dev-tools (partial network overlap).

**The pain:** Buying committees now research vendors through ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews before any sales touch; AI Overviews trigger on 48% of tracked queries as of Feb 2026 (growthspreeofficial.com, grizzle.io); classic SEO traffic decays while content demands rise. Startup-stage SaaS can't afford the $8-25K/mo agencies that dominate the category (npws.net, tripledart.com) and DIY tools fail them (77% of SMBs have no prompting strategy — prior sweep).

**Buyer:** VP Marketing / Head of Growth / founder-CEO. AEO agencies explicitly target VP Marketing titles (yesoptimist.com).

**Budget:** startups invest $3-6K/mo in marketing agencies as the norm; GEO/AI-visibility flat-fee specialists price $3-5K/mo, mid-tier $3-10K/mo (growthlane.marketing, digitaljournal.com). Your $2.5-5K/mo lands in the documented dead zone below agency floors.

**Why 2-person shop:** the verified pricing dead zone ($59/mo tools ↔ $8K+/mo agencies); systems installed on the CLIENT'S stack (they keep the pipelines — anti-lock-in pitch vs agencies); measurable per-workflow ROI framing matches 2026 buyer mood (production rollout, not experiments).

**$0 reach:** LinkedIn build-in-public content (posting machine already exists), warm 2nd-degree intros from fintech network into SaaS founders, communities (Indie Hackers, RevGenius, SaaS Slack groups), the paid-audit tripwire. Slower than ICP #1 — no direct warm layer — expect 2-3 months to first close.

**Role in sequence:** highest repeatability (largest homogeneous segment, standardized Content Engine + AEO System delivery). Land 1-2 crypto/fintech logos first, then use those cases to open SaaS.

## ICP #3 — RU/CIS E-commerce & B2B Services (Mid-Market) — CASH-FLOW LANE

**Segment:** RU e-commerce/D2C and B2B services with high inquiry volume, 20-300 employees, revenue 100M-2B ₽. Geography: Russia (no sanctions exposure for domestic clients).

**The pain (from ru-market sweep, still current):** 53% of small firms already use neural nets in marketing but DIY; 43% don't know where to start, 21% distrust quality — they pay for solved entry points, not tools. OpenAI blocked in RF → managed model access (YandexGPT/GigaChat/DeepSeek via aggregators) is itself sellable. «Продвижение в нейросетях» (GEO) is a formed category: ~15 agencies, retainers 45-300K ₽/mo, median ~150K ₽, legacy SEO players only now entering.

**Buyer:** владелец / директор по маркетингу. **Budget:** single workflow ~35K ₽; paid pilot 100-200K ₽ / 2-4 weeks; retainer 15-80K ₽/mo (mid-market up to 150K ₽/mo for GEO).

**Why 2-person shop:** RU SMBs buy from people, not brands — Telegram personal brand + one VC.ru case article with ROI numbers converts (~50% respond to ROI-numbered cases); big integrators ignore sub-1M ₽ checks.

**$0 reach:** Kwork (Mikhail's seller infra + autopilot already LIVE — unique unfair advantage; AI-agent gigs 80-300K ₽), VC.ru case article, Telegram channels/chats.

**Honest assessment:** fastest path to ANY paying client (Kwork orders already flow), but checks are 3-5x below EN for identical work, upsell ceiling is low, and hours spent here don't build the EN case-study asset. Use it as validation/cash-flow lane capped at ~30% of capacity, not as the strategic ICP.

## ICP #4 — Professional Services EN (Law/Accounting/Consulting) — DEPRIORITIZE

**Segment:** small/mid law firms, accounting, boutique consultancies, US/UK. **Pain is real:** AI platforms are becoming the default interface for high-intent legal queries; AEO is called "the most budget-friendly visibility channel" for small firms; ranked lists of legal-AEO agencies already exist (subscribepr.com, rizeupmedia.com, financialcontent.com). Privacy-sensitive verticals pay premiums (prior sweep: $35K local-LLM deployment).

**Why it ranks last for THIS duo:** zero network into managing partners; trust-heavy, referral-driven buying with long cycles; already served by established legal-marketing agencies bolting on AEO; buyer (managing partner) is culturally distant from two crypto marketers. Speed to first client would likely exceed the 90-day window. Take inbound if it appears; do not spend outbound effort.

## RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE (90 days)

1. **Weeks 1-2:** run free/low-cost AI-visibility teardowns (aeo-audit skill) on 10-15 crypto/fintech B2B targets from the EMCD/LinkedIn network; DM the findings (Telegram/LinkedIn). Simultaneously publish 1-2 Kwork gigs (RU lane) for baseline cash flow.
2. **Weeks 3-8:** convert 1-2 teardowns into paid audits ($1.5-3K) → first retainer ($2.5-5K/mo, AEO system or compliant content engine). Close 1 RU pilot (100-200K ₽) via Kwork/Telegram if it lands without extra sales effort.
3. **Weeks 9-13:** write up the first crypto/fintech case with ROI numbers; start LinkedIn build-in-public targeting B2B SaaS (ICP #2) as the repeatability engine for months 4-9.

Success criteria met if: 1 EN retainer client ($2.5K+/mo) OR 2 RU pilots by day 90 — with the EN retainer being the strategically meaningful outcome.

## Key takeaways
- Lead ICP = crypto/fintech B2B infra (20-200 ppl, seed-Series B, Dubai/EU/global): paid ads are legally choked (Google MiCA-only in EU, Meta 3-tier authorization Mar 2026), making organic + AI-search visibility THE acquisition channel — and the founders' warm EMCD/crypto network makes it the only segment where a first client in 2-6 weeks is realistic.
- AI visibility is already a measured battleground in crypto: Coinbase+Kraken take ~22% of all US crypto AI citations, ChatGPT recommends Coinbase in 85% of exchange queries — every player below the top-5 is invisible and knows it; Mikhail's aeo-audit skill is the natural door-opener.
- Check sizes support $2.5-8K/mo retainers in crypto/fintech: vertical agency retainers run $3-6K entry to $10-50K full-service, so a specialist AEO/content-ops line item is an easy relative sell far below the $20K agency floor.
- ICP #2 (scale engine) = seed-Series B B2B SaaS, 10-100 employees: AI Overviews trigger on 48% of queries, buying committees research via ChatGPT pre-sales, and the verified pricing dead zone ($59/mo tools vs $8-25K/mo agencies) fits a $2.5-5K/mo offer — highest repeatability but 2-3 months to first close, so open it with crypto case studies in month 3+.
- ICP #3 (RU/CIS e-com & B2B services) is the cash-flow lane, not the strategy: Kwork infra already live is an unfair advantage and pilots close fast at 100-200K ₽, but checks are 3-5x below EN for identical work — cap at ~30% of capacity.
- ICP #4 (EN professional services/legal) has real AEO demand but ranks last: no network into managing partners, trust-heavy long cycles, incumbent legal-marketing agencies already bolted on AEO — inbound only.
- Fintech/crypto home turf honest verdict: cyclical budgets, startup churn, and scam-adjacent inbound are real risks (filter to licensed/infra/B2B players, use verify-person), but speed-to-first-client dominates for a 90-day validation goal — it stays #1.
- A differentiated crypto/fintech deliverable exists beyond AEO: compliance-baked-in content pipelines (68% of GenAI-at-scale teams hit QA/compliance bottlenecks within 2 quarters) — generic AI agencies can't produce compliance-safe fintech copy.
- Universal entry motion across ICPs: free teardown DM → paid audit $1.5-3K → $3-5K setup + $2.5-5K/mo retainer; success = 1 EN retainer OR 2 RU pilots by day 90, with the EN retainer as the strategically meaningful proof.
