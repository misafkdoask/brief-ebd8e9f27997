## 5 PATTERN OBSERVATIONS

1. **Stablecoin payments/infra is the hottest and most pitchable sub-segment.** Six funded rounds surfaced in months (KAST $80M, Fun $72M, Velocity $38M, Trace $32M, Range, Checker) plus incumbents BVNK/BCB/Fuze.
