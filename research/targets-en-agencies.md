## Five Observations

1. **PR shops are the widest gap.** Fintech/crypto PR agencies (Caliber, YAP, Wachsman, Ditto, FINPR, MarketAcross, X10) almost universally sell zero productized AI — their "AI" presence is thought-leadership blogging or an AI *client vertical*. They have retainer relationships and reporting pain but no engineering bench at all.

2. **The repeating superficial pattern is a nav-menu line with no depth.** Five targets (William Mills, GuerrillaBuzz, Omni, CSTMR, Flying Elephant) list "AI Search Optimization"/"Discovery Optimization" with no tooling, case studies, or methodology visible. These are the warmest leads: they've already decided to sell the promise and only lack delivery — a white-label deal closes the gap in one call.

3. **The crypto AEO window is closing faster than the fintech one.** Crypto-native competitors (ICODA, Coinpresso) already run mature AI-SEO product lines, which pressures every crypto agency on the list; in fintech PR, nobody comparable showed up. Sequencing: pitch crypto agencies on urgency ("your competitors sell this today"), fintech agencies on greenfield.

4. **Reporting automation is the universal wedge — nobody sells it, everyone pays for it.** Not a single agency of 25 checked productizes client reporting. Paid-media and community-management shops (Growth Gorilla, Lunar, Omni, Cryptorsy, Crowdcreate) carry the heaviest multi-channel reporting load, making the 5-10 h/client/month saving instantly legible to the founder who signs payroll.

5. **Easiest first sale: white-label AEO audit to agencies already selling the promise.** Melrose ("AI Visibility"), William Mills, GuerrillaBuzz and CSTMR have existing line items with no visible delivery machinery — a rev-share audit engine slots in without changing their positioning. Second-easiest: reporting automation to Caliber/Growth Gorilla-type shops, where the pitch is pure cost math rather than a new service launch.
