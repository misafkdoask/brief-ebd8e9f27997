# Service Packaging & Pricing Design — AI Marketing Automation Venture (July 2026)

## 1. Recommended Offer Ladder

A 4-rung ladder: **free wedge → paid audit → fixed-scope pilot sprint → capped retainer**. This mirrors the documented "tripwire audit → retainer" motion in AEO/GEO and the setup-fee + retainer structure that dominates AI automation agency pricing in 2026 ([Thinkpeak](https://thinkpeak.ai/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026/), [Digital Agency Network](https://digitalagencynetwork.com/ai-agency-pricing/)).

### Tier 0 — "AI Visibility Snapshot" (free / near-free lead magnet)
- **Global:** free 1-page teardown (how the prospect appears in ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews for 5-10 buyer queries + 3 obvious fixes). Delivered as Loom or PDF via warm DM.
- **RF:** бесплатный экспресс-чек «как ваш бренд видят нейросети» + одна VC.ru case-статья as inbound engine (proven RF channel per prior sweep).
- Purpose: door-opener only. Cap at 1-2 h each; automate generation from Mikhail's existing aeo-audit skill.

### Tier 1 — Paid Audit (the 90-day validation product)
- **Global: $1,500–$2,500 one-time** (start at $1,500 for first 2 clients, move to $2,500). Market range: audits run $1,000–$5,000 ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/aeo-services-pricing), [Digital Elevator](https://thedigitalelevator.com/blog/aeo-and-geo-pricing-guide/)); enterprise GEO audits go $8,000–$25,000 ([TeamAI](https://teamai.com/blog/generative-ai-and-business/what-is-the-cost-of-geo/)), so $1.5–2.5K is deliberately at the accessible end for SMB/mid-market.
- **RF: 60,000–90,000 ₽** as «GEO-аудит + карта автоматизации». RF anchors: minimum credible GEO entry is 80–100K ₽/mo, market spread 30–500K ₽/mo ([vc.ru pricing breakdown](https://vc.ru/ai/3007956-prodvizhenie-v-neyrosetyah-tseny-i-modeli)); a standalone audit priced below one month of retainer converts best.
- Two flavors, same price: (a) **AEO/GEO Visibility Audit**, (b) **Marketing AI-Ops Audit** (where AI actually cuts cost in their content/SMM/PR stack). Both end with a prioritized roadmap that IS the sales doc for Tier 2.

### Tier 2 — Pilot Automation Sprint (2–4 weeks, fixed scope)
- **Global: $3,500–$6,000 one-time** (first 1-2 clients OK at $3,000). Benchmarks: single-workflow builds $2,500–$15,000, "standard workflow" ~$2,500 floor to $15K production-grade ([Thinkpeak](https://thinkpeak.ai/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026/), [Layer3Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/roi/ai-automation-agency-cost), [MonetizeBot](https://monetizebot.ai/blogs/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026)). Pricing at $3.5–6K undercuts agencies while staying 20-40x above the Fiverr $50–150 floor — sold on senior fintech/crypto operator credibility, not hours.
- **RF: 100,000–200,000 ₽** — exactly the established «платный пилот 100–200К ₽ / 2–4 недели» rung from the RF ladder; RF AI-implementation market confirms: готовые решения от 30K ₽ + настройка, кастом 100–300K ₽ ([vc.ru](https://vc.ru/ai/2925488-stoimost-vnedreniya-ii-v-malyj-biznes), [Sostav](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/281569/86687)).
- Rule: **audit fee credits 50% toward the sprint** if booked within 30 days (standard tripwire mechanics, low cost since audit is templated).

### Tier 3 — Retainer (operate + extend, the real business)
- **Global:**
  - Per-system "run" retainer: **$1,500–$2,500/mo per system** (monitoring, prompt/model updates, iteration). Market: $1,500–$5,000/mo maintenance retainers standard ([Thinkpeak](https://thinkpeak.ai/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026/)); median SMB/mid retainer $2,800–$7,000 ([MonetizeBot](https://monetizebot.ai/blogs/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026)).
  - **AEO/GEO Growth retainer: $2,500–$5,000/mo** — squarely inside the 2026 market band: starter GEO $1,500–$2,500/mo, mid-market $2,000–$8,000/mo, specialists $3,500–$12,000/mo ([Gigawatt Group](https://gigawattgroup.com/insights/geo-aeo-pricing-models-what-agencies-charge-in-2026/), [Fuel Online](https://fuelonline.com/ai-seo-geo/ai-seo-pricing-aeo-geo-cost-2026/), [RevvGrowth](https://www.revvgrowth.com/geo/geo-agency-pricing)). Typical inclusion at this price: 4–6 AEO-optimized posts/mo + schema + prompt testing ([RankAI](https://rankai.ai/articles/what-does-an-ai-seo-agency-charge-monthly)).
  - "AI Marketing Ops" subscription (queue-based, Designjoy-style, one active request): **$3,000–$4,000/mo**, hard-capped at 4-5 clients total. Credible solo-subscription band is $2,500–$7,500/mo ([Brainy Papers](https://brainy.ink/paper/productized-design-services)).
- **RF: 80,000–150,000 ₽/мес** for GEO/AEO (min credible entry 80–100K ₽, median ~150K per RF sweep + [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/ai/3007956-prodvizhenie-v-neyrosetyah-tseny-i-modeli)); **40,000–80,000 ₽/мес** for pure workflow-run retainers (RF support norm 20–30K ₽/mo for chatbots scales up for multi-workflow marketing systems).
- **Pricing asymmetry to exploit:** EN pays 3–5x RUB rates for identical work. Fill capacity global-first; take RF clients when warm (EMCD network) or when they anchor a VC.ru case.

## 2. Package Deliverables — What "Done" Looks Like

### Tier 1 Audit deliverables (5–10 business days)
- **AEO/GEO Visibility Audit:** citation-share report across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/AI Overviews (+ Алиса/YandexGPT for RF) for 20–30 buyer-intent queries; competitor citation benchmark; technical pass (schema/entities, answer-first structure, crawlability for AI bots); off-site citation map (Reddit = 40.1% of LLM citations — include community/PR gaps); prioritized 90-day fix roadmap with effort estimates. **Done = client can see exactly where they lose AI-search demand and what each fix costs.**
- **Marketing AI-Ops Audit:** map of current content/SMM/PR/SEO workflows with hours + cost per asset; 3–5 automation candidates scored by ROI (only where AI actually helps — this is the positioning); tool/model stack recommendation (client-owned accounts); one quick-win implemented live during the audit (e.g., a repurposing prompt chain) as proof. **Done = a costed automation roadmap + one working demo.**

### Tier 2 Sprint deliverables — pick ONE named system per sprint (fixed scope, no "custom AI"):
1. **Content Engine:** n8n/Claude pipeline: brief intake → research → draft in trained brand voice (voice profile built from client corpus) → human review gate → publish/CMS. Done = 8–12 publish-ready long-form assets/mo flowing with <30 min human touch each, brand-voice doc delivered, client team trained (1 session + Loom library).
2. **AEO/GEO Implementation Sprint:** execute audit roadmap top items — schema/entity markup shipped, 5–10 pages restructured answer-first with stats/quotes (documented +22%/+37% citation lift levers), llms-era technical hygiene, monitoring dashboard (Otterly/Peec-class tool on client account, from $29–95/mo). Done = fixes live + baseline citation-share dashboard the retainer will move.
3. **SMM Autopilot:** one long-form input → platform-adapted derivatives (LinkedIn/X/TG for RF) → scheduled via queue with approval step; comment/mention monitoring digest. Done = 20+ posts/mo output from existing content, ≤2 h/week client time.
4. **PR/Outreach System:** monitored source-request feeds (HARO-class, journalist lists), AI-drafted personalized pitches with human send-gate, digital-PR asset (data study) template. Done = 15–30 qualified pitches/mo, pipeline tracked in client CRM/sheet.
- Every sprint ships with: runbook, credentials on client accounts, 2 weeks of post-launch fixes included, and a measured baseline→after metric (hours saved or cost per asset), because ~50% of RF SMBs and outcome-focused EN buyers convert on ROI numbers, not demos.

### Tier 3 Retainer deliverables
- Monthly: system uptime + drift monitoring, prompt/model updates (this is real work — "LLM drift monitoring, prompt versioning" is what $3–15K/mo retainers bill for per [Digital Agency Network](https://digitalagencynetwork.com/ai-agency-pricing/)), 1 improvement iteration per system, monthly report vs. agreed KPI (citation share, cost/asset, output volume). AEO/GEO retainer adds 4–6 optimized assets/mo + off-site citation building. **Done each month = report showing the number moved or an honest flag that it didn't and what changes.**

## 3. Delivery Cost Model at 10–15 h/week per Founder

**Total capacity: 20–30 h/week combined ≈ 90–130 h/month.** Budget 25–30% for sales/marketing/admin (non-negotiable — pipeline death is the #1 side-project failure mode), leaving **~65–95 delivery hours/month**.

Hour costs per unit (after templating; first instance ~2x):
- Audit: 8–12 h (Mikhail's aeo-audit skill + report template compresses this; first ones 15–20 h)
- Sprint: 30–45 h over 2–4 weeks
- Run-retainer: 5–8 h/client/mo; AEO/GEO growth retainer: 10–15 h/client/mo (content is the hour-eater)

**Feasible steady state (month 4–6):** 4–5 retainers (mix of run + 2 AEO/GEO) at ~35–55 h + 1 sprint in flight (~35 h) + 1–2 audits. That is **$10–16K MRR-equivalent at global pricing** — consistent with the solo-operator norm of 3–8 clients with caps and queues ([Assembly](https://assembly.com/blog/productized-services), [How Solos Scale](https://www.howsolosscale.com/p/the-micro-agency-model-how-b2b-solopreneurs)).

**Capacity walls, in order of arrival:**
1. **Concurrent sprints** — never more than 1 (2 only if both are templated systems). Sprint #2 sold while #1 runs goes into a visible queue (queue is a feature: scarcity + Designjoy mechanics).
2. **AEO/GEO content hours** — each growth retainer at 10–15 h/mo means the wall is ~3 such clients; past that, either raise price or productize content production harder.
3. **Meetings/timezones** — async-first delivery (Loom + written reports) or the 10-15 h/week budget evaporates into calls. One 30-min call/client/mo max.
4. **Founder-skill coupling** — if only Mikhail can touch n8n/Claude Code and only Anna can do client comms, either founder's busy week stalls everything. Cross-document from day one.

**Systematize first (in order):**
1. Audit → report generator (highest leverage: it's the sales engine; target <8 h/audit by audit #3)
2. Library of 4 named-system n8n/agent templates — 70% reusable core, 30% client config ("small menu of repeatable templates" is exactly the Saraev/LeftClick $70K+/mo pattern)
3. Client reporting automation (auto-pulled metrics → monthly report draft) — retainer margin lives or dies here
4. Onboarding intake (form + credential checklist + kickoff doc) — saves 3–5 h per new client
- **Pricing-to-capacity rule:** price so that 4–5 clients hit the revenue goal; needing 8–10 clients means you've built an agency you can't staff ([Brainy Papers](https://brainy.ink/paper/productized-design-services)).

## 4. Contract & Positioning Tips

**Pricing structure: fixed-scope + flat retainer, outcome-FRAMED but not outcome-PRICED (yet).**
- 38% of US agencies moved to retainer-plus-performance or outcome pricing, and it aligns incentives — but attribution is "the hardest scoping problem in the business" ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-pricing-models-2026-decision-guide)). With no track record, pure outcome pricing = unpaid risk. Instead: flat fee + named KPI in every SOW ("cost per published asset from $X to $Y", "citation share on 25 queries from A% to B%") + optional small success bonus once trust exists. Retainer-heavy agencies run ~8 points higher net margins than project-based peers ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-pricing-models-2026-decision-guide)) — the ladder exists to reach retainers.
- Never sell hours. Flat-fee is itself a differentiator against platform credit-billing chaos (HubSpot per-resolution, Copy.ai credit-burn complaints).

**Scope creep controls (Karl Sakas: the single biggest profit leak in fixed-fee work, 5-7 figures/yr at agencies — [Agency Dashboard](https://agencydashboard.io/blog/agency-pricing-models)):**
- Sell **named systems, not "automation help"**. SOW lists the system, its inputs/outputs, integration count, revision rounds (2), and an explicit "not included" list.
- Retainer = maintain + iterate the *named* systems. New system = new sprint at sprint price. Pre-write the change-order template; quote add-ons in 24 h so "no" never has to be said, only "yes, here's the price."
- Queue-based subscription: one active request at a time, pause/cancel anytime. Never say "unlimited" — it killed most Designjoy copycats.
- **API cost pass-through:** client pays model/tool costs on their own keys (OpenAI/Anthropic; ProxyAPI/YandexGPT/GigaChat for RF). Agent workloads show 5–10x token variance between easy and hard runs — absorbing that inside a flat retainer wipes out margin ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/agent-pricing-models-token-vs-outcome-based-2026)). Passing it through also removes the trust problem clients have with credit billing.

**IP ownership of built automations — recommended split (and a selling point):**
- **Client owns what runs:** on full payment, the deployed workflows, prompts, configs, data and accounts are theirs; everything runs on client-owned infrastructure (their n8n instance — self-hostable by design, their model keys, their CMS). "It keeps running whether or not you keep working with us" is exactly the contract language buyers now look for; "no mention of who owns code/accounts at project end" is a listed red flag in 2026 buyer guides ([Uplift](https://getuplift.ai/blog/ai-automation-agency), [LumaLex](https://www.lumalexlaw.com/2026/06/29/who-owns-the-ai-tools-your-employees-are-building/)). Lead with this against agency lock-in fear — it converts.
- **Venture retains:** pre-existing IP + generic/reusable components, templates, prompt patterns and know-how, granted to the client as a perpetual non-exclusive license within their delivered systems. This clause is what lets the template library (the future product per the services-to-product sweep) legally accumulate across clients. Explicitly written as "Contractor background IP / reusable components" — standard, uncontroversial when stated up front.
- No exclusivity, no non-compete on the venture side (you will redeploy the same Content Engine skeleton for the next client).
- **Handoff insurance:** every sprint includes a runbook + training so the client *can* operate alone — most churn-risk retainers survive because clients *choose* not to. The known agency failure mode is "team inherits something they didn't design and don't understand" ([Uplift](https://getuplift.ai/blog/ai-automation-agency)); documented handoff paradoxically increases retention and referrals.
- **RF specifics:** contract via самозанятость/ИП with a licensing annex; client data stays on RF-compliant models where 152-ФЗ matters (YandexGPT); the managed-model-access problem (OpenAI blocked) is itself billable setup work.

**Positioning language that matches 2026 buyer mood** (production rollout + governance, post-hype): sell "we install and operate a measured AI marketing system on YOUR stack, you own everything, flat fee, per-workflow ROI" — the direct antidote to the Gartner-predicted 40% agentic-project cancellation wave and to the sub-$3K relabeled-SEO "GEO" offers the market already distrusts.

## 90-Day Application
Weeks 1–2: publish the 4 named-system one-pager + audit sales page; build audit template. Weeks 2–6: 10–15 warm DMs (LinkedIn network + EMCD alumni + fintech/crypto ICP), sell 2–3 audits at $1,500 / 60–90K ₽. Weeks 4–10: convert 1–2 audits into sprints ($3.5–6K / 100–200K ₽) with the 50% audit credit. Weeks 8–13: convert sprint #1 into the first retainer. Two paying clients = 2 audits + 1 sprint ≈ **$6.5–9K revenue inside 90 days at ~60–80 total delivery hours** — comfortably inside the 10–15 h/week envelope.

## Key takeaways
- Ladder: free AI-visibility snapshot → paid audit ($1,500-2,500 / 60-90K ₽) → 2-4 wk pilot sprint ($3,500-6,000 / 100-200K ₽) → retainer ($1,500-2,500/mo per system, $2,500-5,000/mo AEO/GEO growth; RF 80-150K ₽/mo) — every price point sits inside verified 2026 market bands.
- Sell 4 named systems only (Content Engine, AEO/GEO Implementation, SMM Autopilot, PR/Outreach System) with explicit done-criteria and a measured baseline→after KPI; never sell generic 'AI automation'.
- Capacity at 20-30 combined h/week ≈ 65-95 delivery h/mo: steady state is 4-5 retainers + 1 sprint in flight ≈ $10-16K MRR at global pricing; walls are concurrent sprints (max 1), AEO/GEO content hours (max ~3 growth retainers), and meeting load (go async-first).
- Systematize in this order: audit report generator (<8 h by audit #3), 4 reusable n8n/agent system templates (70% shared core), automated client reporting, onboarding intake — the template library is also the future product IP.
- Price flat-fee, outcome-framed but not outcome-priced: attribution risk makes pure performance pricing a trap for a new shop; flat fees also differentiate against distrusted platform credit billing.
- Pass model/API costs through on client-owned keys (OpenAI/Anthropic global; ProxyAPI/YandexGPT/GigaChat for RF) — 5-10x token variance otherwise eats flat-retainer margin, and it defuses billing distrust.
- IP split: client owns deployed workflows/prompts/accounts on their own infra (self-hosted n8n, their keys) on full payment — sell this openly as anti-lock-in; venture retains background IP and reusable templates via a non-exclusive license-back clause.
- Scope creep is the #1 profit leak: fixed SOW per named system, 2 revision rounds, pre-written change-order priced within 24 h, queue-based one-active-request subscription, never the word 'unlimited'.
- Global-first fills capacity (EN pays 3-5x RUB for identical work); RF clients taken when warm (EMCD network) or as VC.ru case anchors at the established 100-200K ₽ pilot rung.
- 90-day validation math works: 2-3 audits + 1 sprint ≈ $6.5-9K revenue in ~60-80 delivery hours, converting sprint #1 into the first retainer by week 13.
